Time-Series Validation Protocol

Standard random k-fold cross-validation or shuffling was strictly avoided to prevent temporal leakage. The latest 20% of eligible monthly observations from each state (22 months, March 2023 to December 2024; 66 total) were withheld as a forward-looking test set. Training was conducted on the earlier 86 months (January 2016 to February 2023; 258 total). All lag features (1, 2, 3, 12 months) and moving averages (3-month and 6-month) were derived exclusively from preceding periods.

Performance Discussion & Baselines

Linear Regression yielded an MAE of 9,730.60 and an R² of 0.7497 on the holdout test set. While the naive "Last Month" baseline achieves a competitive single-step MAE (9,623.08) due to autoregressive momentum, multi-step recursive testing reveals that it quickly degrades (MAE > 32,000 at 3 and 6 months) because it lacks seasonal dynamics. Linear Regression preserves lower error bounds and generalizability across expanding folds.
